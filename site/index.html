How this was built

  • Every morning, scan Redfin's new-construction listings in 105 cities where builders are active, keeping homes on the market 60+ days.
  • Read each home's full price history and take its highest asking price since it was last sold (or the last two years).
  • Drop one-day price glitches, duplicate feeds, land, unbuilt plans and mobile homes; list only homes with at least three step-down cuts.
  • The trend charts use the original 40 cities only, so each day compares with the last.

Before you dance

  • A big drop can mean the first ask was too high, not that the house is cheap. Compare against recent sales nearby.
  • Builders often add rate buydowns or closing credits on top of the cut. Ask what's offered.
  • Perry Homes shows up again and again in Austin and Dallas suburbs; one seller cutting this hard may cut more.
  • Prices change daily. Treat this as a starting list, not advice; I'm not a licensed financial advisor.
